🎣 How Phishing Works
  1. The lure — an urgent email, SMS or call impersonating a brand you trust.
  2. The pressure — a deadline, a threat, or a prize to stop you thinking.
  3. The trap — a link to a pixel-perfect fake login page.
  4. The harvest — you type your credentials or OTP straight to the attacker.
  5. The replay — they log into the real site as you, within seconds.
🔑
The one rule that stops most phishing: never enter credentials on a page you reached by clicking a link. Type the address yourself, or use your bookmark.
