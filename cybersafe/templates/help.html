🚨
Been scammed? Act in this order, right now.
1. Call 1930 (India national cyber financial fraud helpline) — within the golden hour the money can often be frozen. 2. Report at cybercrime.gov.in. 3. Call your bank to block the card/account. 4. Change passwords from a different, clean device. 5. Preserve all screenshots and transaction references as evidence.
🚀 Getting Started
  1. Install Ollama from ollama.com — free and open source.
  2. Run ollama serve in a terminal, then ollama pull llama3.2.
  3. Start this platform with python app.py and open http://127.0.0.1:5000.
  4. Check the AI status pill in the top-right corner — it should read AI Online.
  5. New to the field? Start at Learning Center → Cyber Safety Essentials.
💡
Ollama is optional. Every detection engine — phishing heuristics, password maths, malware signatures, log detections, forensic parsers — works fully offline without it. The AI adds explanation and nuance on top.

⚖️ Legal & Ethical Use
⚠️
Only examine systems and data you own or are formally authorised to examine. Unauthorised access to a computer or its data is a criminal offence under the Information Technology Act, 2000 in India, and under equivalent laws elsewhere.
🎓
This platform is built for education, personal safety and authorised investigation. It is deliberately defensive: it contains no exploit code, no attack tooling, and the AI refuses to produce malware.
📋
For evidence intended for court, findings from this educational tool should be reproduced and corroborated by a certified forensic examiner using validated commercial or established open-source tooling.
